Minka OS

Ideas, memory and work. Together.

A personal AI workspace being developed to carry context across projects, use reusable skills and make progress easier to understand.

Ask about the project

A clearer view of the work.

The current revamp focuses on model management, reliable task execution and a dashboard that shows what has happened, what needs attention and what is ready to review.

From references to a proposal.

One planned workflow turns a business’s Instagram presence or supplied images into a visual brief and a private website proposal. The playbook is in place; visual intake, preview integration and end-to-end validation are still being developed.

A familiar presence.

The design direction pairs a recognizable Minka/Aura avatar with a subtle holographic treatment. A phone-facing vault is planned for capturing ideas and recalling or updating them through conversation.

Minka OS is an internal development project. The redesigned dashboard, 3D avatar and complete proposal workflow are not available as a public product.
