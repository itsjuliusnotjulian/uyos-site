Privacy

Last updated October 24, 2026

AnamnesisYouTube and Google dataThis website

Your memories stay under your control. We do not sell your personal data, show ads or track you across apps and websites. Photo-library access is for features on your phone, not permission to upload your photos or videos. Optional services that process conversation text require a separate choice after an explanation of what is sent and why. Read the details below, or write to hello@uyos.io.

Anamnesis

TestFlight versions: the separate default-off online-voice consent controls described here apply from build 39. Build 40 removes the Apple synthesized-voice fallback entirely; recorded Aura/Minka speech works offline and new spoken replies need the optional familiar voice. Build 39 may still use an Apple voice fallback. Builds 37–38 may use the voice service automatically when available; update before relying on local-only reply speech. Accepting the general terms does not enable either optional service in build 39 or later.

  • Personal questions: when enabled, Apple Intelligence on this iPhone can use passages from your saved stories and conversation notes to make a new question. Recent memories and rotating older ones provide context; repeated questions are checked against the book and prompt history. If the local model is unavailable, the app uses its question collection. This feature does not send your book to a question-generation server. Turn it off in Settings for collection-only questions.
  • Private and difficult conversations: in Talk with me, choose Keep this conversation private or ask to keep it private. This choice applies to the whole current conversation: it is left out of the book and future conversation notes. Recorded privacy acknowledgments play locally. From build 44, new private replies appear as text instead of repeatedly playing the same recorded reassurance. Private text is not sent to the optional online voice. When the companion recognizes a difficult topic, it asks about privacy before keeping it; an unanswered privacy choice leaves the conversation out. Recordings and typed memories also offer a private option. Difficult memories you explicitly choose to keep remain in the book but are not automatically resurfaced or used for new questions. These conversation cues are not health assessments and cannot recognize every private or difficult topic; the private control remains available. Private chats still use temporary on-device microphone buffers while speaking, which are discarded when stopped. Existing saved memories are not erased by making a later conversation private.
  • Repeated memories: the app checks new book entries against existing story text. An exact repeat is left out; a close match can be kept if you say it adds something new. Different wording can escape these checks. You can talk about a memory repeatedly without needing another book page.
  • Your welcome: a short greeting follows this iPhone's local time. The chosen name is displayed; speaking a new personalized greeting requires the optional online familiar voice. Holiday greetings default to secular; optional Christian greetings and a celebration name/date you choose stay in your local settings. No religion is inferred. If you enable the optional online voice below, the resulting greeting text passes through that service too. The welcome does not turn on the microphone.
  • What it keeps: your name, the interests you pick, an optional photo of yourself, your answers (voice recordings, the words written down from them, and anything you type), photos you choose to tell stories about, and your reminder time.
  • Where: your book, conversation notes and recordings are kept on your iPhone. New camera pictures and videos you choose to keep go to Photos; Through the years keeps references to them. A profile picture and photos attached to stories may also have local copies in the app. Uyos does not receive or sell your photo library, photos or videos. Optional text-processing and encrypted Alexa delivery are described below; they are not blanket permission to send other data.
  • Speech to text: your words are written down using Apple's on-device speech recognition where your iPhone supports it and you allow it. Otherwise you can still record a story without automatic transcription. Story and companion audio is not sent to a speech-transcription server.
  • Microphone: used while you are telling a story, talking with Anamnesis, recording a video, humming a song or identifying music, while the relevant screen is open. If you enable hands-free replies, it also listens for up to eight seconds after a browsing invitation, using on-iPhone speech recognition. Listening stops when you scroll away, leave the page or leave the app. An answer to a memory question can continue into its recording page, keeping your first words; a navigation-only reply is discarded.
  • Conversation continuity from build 46: New dialogue is generated with Apple’s on-device FoundationModels when available. The conversation labels Apple-generated replies separately from recorded controls and factual reminders quoted from your earlier words. Relevant user statements are used as local context; new future-memory notes must quote your words exactly. Earlier notes without that grounding are excluded from automatic question seeding, without deleting your book. If the separate local metadata check fails, that reply remains local text rather than being sent to the optional voice service. Private-conversation exclusions and your separate voice consent still apply.
  • Your photo library (if you allow it): Anamnesis looks through the photos and videos on your iPhone to find older ones to ask you about, shows small copies of them moving behind the day's photo, and plays a video on your screen when one comes up (only videos already on the phone; nothing is downloaded or uploaded). It reads each photo's date and what's in it (how many people, a beach, a wedding) using Apple's image recognition on the iPhone itself. Photo prompts can choose a picture automatically from the photos you allow, with an option to choose your own. Photo-library access does not automatically upload photos. Photos you attach to stories or deliberately shared memories can have local app copies. Explicit file sharing is described below. You can turn this off in Settings → Anamnesis → Photos.
  • Camera and microphone for pictures and videos: the microphone and camera stop when you leave the app; recording does not continue over other apps. Open the camera to take pictures, selfies, videos with sound or scans. Classic and fun filters run on your phone. Kept captures are saved to Photos, and existing library originals are never edited, moved or deleted by Anamnesis. Removing a story leaves its photo in Photos. Photos syncing through your own iCloud Photos settings is controlled by Apple and your settings, not an upload to Uyos.
  • Music (only if you tap Connect my music): Anamnesis then uses Apple Music to search for songs as you type, play the songs you choose, list your playlists so you can add one, and suggest songs you've been playing. Your song list, playlists and listening history are read on your phone and never sent to us. To disconnect: Settings, Anamnesis, Media & Apple Music.
  • Humming a song: if you hum or sing a song you remember, it's saved as a story in your book, on your phone, like any other recording.
  • "What's this song?" (if you use it): when you tap it, Anamnesis listens for a few seconds and uses Apple's ShazamKit to name the song playing near you. Only a short sound fingerprint is sent to Apple to find the match, never a recording, and it listens only while that screen is open. See Apple's privacy policy for Shazam.
  • Local model choices from build 47: Settings lets you choose Apple Intelligence (the initial default), Qwen3.5 2B or Qwen3.5 4B for Let’s Talk. Your choice applies to new conversations; the app does not silently substitute another model. Qwen requires an explicit one-time Wi-Fi download from Hugging Face; that provider and its download infrastructure receive the requested model identifier and ordinary connection information such as your IP address, never conversation text, photos, recordings or your book. Model files are pinned and verified on your phone, stored outside backups, and removable in Settings. Once downloaded, model inference and its separate privacy/memory metadata check use local files and run on the phone without a chat server or implicit model download. Replies identify the selected engine and elapsed reply time. Current-location tools and reminders quoted from your earlier words have separate source labels. Other photo and adaptive-question features continue to use Apple Intelligence. Optional online familiar speech remains separately consented; private or unclassified new replies remain local text. Model choice does not automatically upload memories or use them to train a remote model. Conversation quality and physical-device memory/performance remain beta limitations; 4B may be too demanding on some phones.
  • Current-location questions from build 47: Asking Talk where you are requests one foreground, approximate location with your iPhone permission, then sends that location to Apple’s place-name service. Only a city, region or country is shown in the reply; raw coordinates and addresses do not enter the conversation, book or future-memory notes. No location trail is kept. The lookup stops when you leave Talk or the app goes into the background. A denied, stale or unavailable fix is not guessed. This works independently of Apple Intelligence and the optional weather/nearby switches. Even in a private conversation, an explicit location question uses Apple’s place lookup; conversation text is not sent to Apple for it. A non-private city/region reply may pass through the online familiar voice if you enabled it; private replies remain local text. Revoke Location for Anamnesis in iPhone Settings to prevent lookups.
  • Weather questions (off unless you turn them on): on a rainy, snowy or hot day, Anamnesis can ask about a day like it. When this is on, it uses your approximate location (your town, not your address) once a day to check the weather with Apple Weather. The location isn't stored, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings, or turn off location for Anamnesis in Settings.
  • Browsing invitations: Anamnesis uses which card is centered on screen to highlight it and offer a spoken invitation. You can turn the invitations off in Settings. There is no saved browsing history or analytics. Spoken invitation text passes through the same voice service described under Talk with me.
  • Nearby memory ideas (off until enabled): while the app is open, Anamnesis can use your location to look up nearby parks, campgrounds, museums and similar places with Apple Maps, then suggest a picture or a story after a quiet stop. Apple receives the area needed for this lookup. Coordinates stay transient on your phone; no route or location history is saved or sent to us. Only the last suggested place name and date are kept on your phone to avoid repeats. A place name may pass through the spoken-reply service if an invitation is spoken, and a story you choose to record can include that name. Location updates stop when you leave the app. Turn this off in the app's Settings, or revoke location access in iPhone Settings.
  • Birthday reminders (only if you allow contacts): Anamnesis reads the birthdays saved in your contacts, with the name and the phone number or email, so it can remind you on the day and offer to send a "Happy birthday" message or call. Contacts are read on your phone and never sent to us or anyone else. Messages are sent only by you, from your phone. You can turn off access in Settings, Anamnesis, Contacts.
  • Talk with me: speech recognition and supported conversation generation run on the iPhone. Short conversation notes stay there so the app can follow up another day. From build 44, the app checks for repeated replies/questions and offers an explicit retry if it cannot find a fresh answer, rather than cycling canned replies. Names are reserved for the opening greeting. Recorded questions play locally, and from build 40 no Apple synthesized-voice fallback is used. New spoken replies require the familiar voice to be enabled; otherwise recorded phrases play and other replies remain as text. The online familiar voice is optional and off by default: before enabling it, you see that reply text may contain names or details from your conversation. If enabled, reply text plus a generated phone identifier and access token pass to our voice service, which returns speech audio without saving the reply text or audio. No photos, videos, existing book or microphone recordings are sent to that service. Turn it off in Settings to stop new requests; Forget voice-service connection removes its relay registration when Alexa does not also use it. A request already sent cannot be recalled. No conversation recording is kept by this feature; a part you choose to keep becomes a local page in your book.
  • Alexa (only if you link it): Alexa conversations are a separate opt-in, off by default. Before enabling and linking, you see that words spoken to the Echo pass through Amazon and our relay to produce a reply. The relay processes those words temporarily, then keeps encrypted answers for your iPhone until collection or for up to 30 days. They are deleted from the relay on collection; only your iPhone holds the private key needed to decrypt the stored answers. The relay also retains a generated phone identifier, a hash of the access token, a public encryption key and the Alexa account identifier needed for linking. Photos, videos and the existing book on your iPhone are not uploaded to Alexa or the relay. Unlink in Settings to remove the relay registration, link and waiting answers. Amazon separately handles voice recordings and session data under its own settings and privacy policy; unlinking here does not delete Amazon's records. Nothing is sold.
  • Consent is specific: enabling the online voice does not enable Alexa, photo access does not authorize media uploads, and neither service gives permission to sell data. You can use the core app without either online service. Each optional feature explains its purpose and the data it needs before you turn it on.
  • Games: Anamnesis keeps a record of each game on your phone (the level, how long it took and how many tries) so it can show your levels and best times and open new games. It stays on your phone, isn't sent to us or anyone else, and isn't used to judge health.
  • Reminders and the Home Screen widget: the daily reminder (only if you allow notifications) and the optional widget show the day's question on your iPhone, including on the Lock Screen depending on your notification settings. They can show the day's question and a photo thumbnail from your allowed library, never your recorded or written story answers. Turn reminders off in Settings, or remove the widget like any other.
  • Help from family: you choose one contact to call when you need help. iOS gives the app only that one contact (a name and a phone number or email), never your address book. "I need help" starts a normal FaceTime call; any screen sharing happens in FaceTime, which you control.
  • Shared memories (from build 43, off by default): enable this separately in My Book or Settings. Choose or capture a photo, a title, date and shared words; your chosen name is included. Saving makes a local page. Tapping Share this memory then opens the iPhone share sheet; only that explicit action sends the memory file through the service you choose. The file contains a resized full-frame JPEG and those chosen details, not other stories, private conversations, recordings or your contacts. Re-encoding leaves out original photo EXIF/GPS metadata. Uyos does not receive or relay the file. AirDrop, Messages, Files or another delivery service may store/process it under their own settings. The recipient reviews it and explicitly adds a local page; their added perspective stays in their own book. Re-sharing uses the original shared fields, excluding later private additions. Duplicate imports are checked by shared identity. Turning the feature off stops new imports/shares but does not remove existing book pages, revoke delivered files or erase copies someone else accepted. Deleting a page changes only that local book. No address-book upload, automatic contact matching or cloud book synchronization is used.
  • Sending a story: when you tap "Send to family", your iPhone's share sheet sends that one story the way you choose (Messages, Mail). Nothing is sent unless you do this.
  • Smart questions: on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Anamnesis may ask a follow-up question and title a story using Apple's on-device model. It runs on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Siri: if you use Anamnesis's Siri phrases, Siri hears only the command (for example "record my story"); your story is recorded by the app on your iPhone. See Apple's privacy policy for Siri.
  • The keepsake book: Anamnesis keeps a copy of the whole book (a web page with the recordings, and a PDF) on your iPhone, so your family can keep it someday. If you or your family turn on "Also keep the book in iCloud Drive", a copy is saved in a folder you choose in your own iCloud Drive. It's off unless you turn it on. Downloading the whole book asks for the iPhone's passcode or Face ID first.
  • Backups and deleting: if your iPhone backs up to iCloud, the app's stories are part of your own backup, under your Apple Account. Delete a story in the app, or delete the app to remove everything from the iPhone.
  • Children: Anamnesis is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

AuraDeepDive publishing and Google user data

Uyos uses a private publishing tool for its own AuraDeepDive channel (what it is). When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to manage that channel's videos and to read its analytics and comments.

  • What it accesses: the signed-in channel's videos, playlists, thumbnails, comments and analytics, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.
  • How it's used: only to upload and manage Uyos's own videos and to report on the channel's performance to Uyos. Nothing else.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is kept in a file only the tool's user account can read, on Uyos's own computer, whose disk is encrypted. Channel data is used to answer a request and is not kept beyond Uyos's own records of its videos.
  • Sharing: Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with third parties and never used to train AI models.
  • Limited Use: the tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token stops working immediately. To have it deleted too, write to hello@uyos.io.
  • The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
