Anamnesis

  • It doesn't hear me: on the iPhone, open Settings → Anamnesis and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition.
  • No daily reminder: Settings → Notifications → Anamnesis → Allow Notifications.
  • Listening to a story: open My Book and tap the page.
  • Something else: write to us with what happened and, if you can, a screenshot.
