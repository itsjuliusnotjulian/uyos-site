Keep an iCloud backup of your iPhone turned on, and keep the keepsake book somewhere safe. You're responsible for backing up your data; Uyos isn't responsible for data that is lost or damaged.

Not an emergency or medical service

Anamnesis keeps stories and keeps you company. It is not an emergency, medical, or monitoring service and doesn't watch over anyone's safety or health. "I need help" starts an ordinary FaceTime call to the person you chose; whether they answer is up to them. The "Are you OK?" check only works while Anamnesis is open on the screen; it can miss a fall or sound for a dropped phone, it can't place a call or send a message by itself, and it is no substitute for Apple's Emergency SOS or Apple Watch Fall Detection. In an emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number).

Questions and suggestions

Recorded speech plays on your iPhone. Starting with build 39, other replies use an installed iPhone voice by default. The online familiar voice and Alexa conversations are separate optional services, both off until you enable them after their data-use explanation. Reply text may include personal details and passes through the enabled voice service without the service saving the text or audio. Alexa processes words spoken to the Echo and uses temporary encrypted delivery as described in the privacy policy; Amazon has its own data settings. No photo or video upload permission is included in either opt-in. You can turn these connections off in Settings. Network access is needed only for those online features; the privacy policy identifies other optional connections and older TestFlight behavior. See our privacy policy for how each works.

Some questions are written by Apple's on-device model from what the phone knows about a photo or a story. They can be wrong or miss the point; they're invitations to remember, not facts.

On supported iPhones, optional personal questions use saved words locally and may misunderstand them. The companion can acknowledge difficult feelings and offer gentler memories, but is not therapy and does not establish whether remembered events occurred. Private conversation controls apply to the current Talk with me session and prevent it being added to the book or future notes. They do not delete earlier saved pages or change Amazon's separate handling of conversations on an Echo. Review what you choose to keep; similarity checks can miss repeated stories.
